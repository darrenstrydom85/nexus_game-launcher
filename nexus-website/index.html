The full list

Every feature, sorted.

  • Auto-import LibrarySteam, Epic, GOG, Ubisoft Connect, Battle.net and Xbox, detected automatically.
  • Your own folders LibraryWatch a folder and the standalone games inside it show up.
  • Rich metadata LibraryCovers, descriptions, genres and critic ratings from IGDB.
  • Instant launch LibraryPress Play and the right launcher starts in the background.
  • Game pages LibraryRatings, How Long to Beat, screenshots, similar games and Twitch.
  • Quick view LibraryA drawer slides in beside the library with the essentials.
  • Archive LibraryKeep track of games after they’re uninstalled.
  • Play time TrackingAccurate sessions across every source.
  • Stats TrackingSide-by-side cards, comparisons with the last period and an activity chart.
  • Streaks TrackingA daily play streak with a heatmap of your play.
  • Mastery tiers TrackingBronze to Diamond for every game you put hours into.
  • Achievements and XP TrackingNative achievements, rarity and a launcher-wide level.
  • Wrapped TrackingYour month or year as a story you step through and share.
  • Collections OrganiseHand-picked shelves you can drag to reorder.
  • Smart collections OrganiseRules that keep a collection up to date for you.
  • Search and filters OrganiseCtrl K from anywhere. Filter by source, status, genre, score or mastery.
  • Up next OrganiseA play queue you can reorder.
  • Notes and tags OrganiseNotes per game and colour-coded tags across the library.
  • Completed shelf OrganiseEvery game you’ve finished, in one place.
  • Twitch ConnectWho’s live for your games, trending in your library, channel pages and clips.
  • How Long to Beat ConnectTime to beat and what’s left, based on your own play.
  • SteamGridDB ConnectSwap covers, logos and hero art.
  • Google Drive backup ConnectEncrypted backups to your own Drive, on your schedule.
  • Open a pack ExperienceRip open a pack and flip a card to pick your next game.
  • Theme Studio Experience14 presets, any accent colour, fonts, text size and corner radius.
  • Retro mode ExperienceTurn the library into a DOS-era terminal, one click away.
  • Local-first ExperienceNo account. Your library lives in one SQLite file on your PC.
  • Works offline ExperienceCached art and data when you’re disconnected.
  • System tray ExperienceMinimise to the tray and launch games from it.
